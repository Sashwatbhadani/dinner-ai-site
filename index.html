All Commands

Complete list of available commands

💬

AI Chat Commands

/ask Chat with Dinner AI and get intelligent, fun responses.
🔥

Fun Commands

/roast Roast a selected server member with funny, savage Hinglish AI-generated roasts.
📈

Global Stock Market Commands

/register Create your global Dinner AI financial account and receive 10,000 DC.
/stockmarket View the global stock exchange information.
/companies View all 25 companies and their current share prices.
/buy Purchase company shares using DC.
/sell Sell your owned company shares.
/balance Check your Dinner AI Currency balance.
/portfolio View your shares, investments and portfolio value.
/networth Check your total net worth, including wallet and shares.
/global-leaderboard View the richest investors across the global economy.
⚙️

Server Setup

/setup-dinner-ai Configure Dinner AI in the current channel. Only server administrators can configure it.
🧠

Memory Commands

/reset-memory Clear your personal conversation history and start fresh.
🛡️

Moderation Commands

/ban Ban a member using their User ID.
/unban Unban a member using their User ID.
/kick Kick a member from the server.
📩

Support Commands

/report-issue Report a problem with Dinner AI.
/suggestions Send your suggestions to the Dinner AI team.

📌 Important Information

  • Dinner AI works in the configured channel for AI chat.
  • Conversation memory is individual for each member.
  • Use /reset-memory whenever you want to clear your history.
  • Use /roast to generate funny Hinglish roasts.
  • Global stock prices update every 30 minutes when a market command triggers an update.
  • All investors share the same global market.
  • The starting balance for new investors is 10,000 DC.
  • All stock-market transactions use Dinner AI Currency (DC).
